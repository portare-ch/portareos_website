What this is

The inventory of what the SM8550 build sets up: every folder the launcher lists, what goes in it, and the one emulator that runs it. It is generated from the build's own system list (SUPPORTED_EMULATORS_AND_CORES.md, written by the build from virtual/emulators/package.mk), not read off the recipe by eye.

PortareOS on SM8550
Console and computer systems44
libretro cores under RetroArch13
Standalone emulators and launchers8
Systems with more than one emulator3, all SNES: bsnes is still under evaluation beside Snes9x

One emulator per system. Where ROCKNIX shipped several for a platform, one was picked and the rest dropped, together with the settings nobody had tuned for them. The cores are bsnes, Dolphin, FBNeo, Flycast, Gambatte, Genesis Plus GX, mGBA, NeoCD, ParaLLEl N64, PicoDrive, PPSSPP, Snes9x, SwanStation. The standalones are ARMSX2, gmu, Moonlight, mpv, PortMaster, ScummVM, Steam, xemu.

What went

Everything that duplicated one of the emulators above, or ran a system this handheld is not for: the standalone builds of Dolphin, Flycast, PPSSPP and DuckStation beside their cores; ares and Mednafen; the second and third N64 emulators; RPCS3; Wine, Heroic and the Windows system; and the long tail of single-system emulators inherited from ROCKNIX. What remains is the table above, and each entry in it is the one that was configured for this panel.
